Rider FAQ

Do I need to pay anything upfront?

No down payment. You pay your first rent and a refundable deposit at collection, and we confirm the amount on the call.

What happens if my EV breaks down?

Call our service line. We guide you, send help or give you another EV if the repair takes time.

Can I use the EV for any delivery app?

Yes. Ride for any platform you like.

Can I stop my plan?

Yes. Give the notice your plan requires, return the EV in working condition, and get your deposit back after inspection.

Am I safe while riding?

Our support team is one call away, and we can see your ride live.
